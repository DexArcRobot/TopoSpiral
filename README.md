# Topology-Preserving Scalar Field Optimization for Boundary-Conforming Spiral Toolpaths on Multiply Connected Freeform Surfaces

This repository contains the core implementation of the methods presented in our paper, “Topology-Preserving Scalar Field Optimization for Boundary-Conforming Spiral Toolpaths on Multiply Connected Freeform Surfaces.”

This project is developed by DexArcRobot (灵弧机器人), a research team at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), led by Prof. Han Ding and Prof. Xiaojian Zhang. Dr. Changqing Shen is responsible for algorithm implementation and software development.

Our research focuses on robotic machining, geometric computing, and motion planning. We apply geometric methods and optimization algorithms to improve the efficiency and quality of machining complex surfaces.

By sharing our research code, we aim to support reproducible research, academic exchange, and engineering applications. We welcome researchers and developers to build upon, extend, and improve this work.
